---
name: Atmosphere honesty boundary
description: Rules for generated discovery content and its separation from real social activity.
---

Atmosphere may generate clearly labeled route, motivation, world, Hub-search, Pace, and map discovery prompts only after prioritizing real user content and legitimate external discovery.

**Why:** Generated material must make quiet surfaces useful without inventing users, followers, reactions, comments, transactions, room participants, LIVE viewers, achievements, or activity metrics.

**How to apply:** Keep LIVE entirely real. Label generated cards as discovery, avoid human-post affordances, preserve their generated origin through ranking and analytics, and reduce their share as legitimate activity grows.
