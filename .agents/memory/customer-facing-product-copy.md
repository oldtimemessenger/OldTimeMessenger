---
name: Customer-facing product copy
description: Language boundary for text shown inside Old Time.
---

Customer-facing Old Time screens must describe what people can do and what will happen. Do not mention servers, APIs, providers, services, backends, caches, or unfinished infrastructure.

**Why:** Internal implementation language makes the app feel unfinished and confusing, especially during App Store review.

**How to apply:** Rewrite notices, empty states, errors, and settings descriptions in familiar product language. Keep technical detail in logs and developer-only diagnostics.
