---
name: Updates news boundaries
description: Product boundary for personalized Updates feeds and future licensed news ingestion.
---

The Updates experience may learn from opens, likes, saves, comments, shares, hides, chosen interests, language, and usage patterns, but provider ingestion, automated briefs, translations, and licensed imagery must be shown as inactive until real services are connected.

**Why:** Attribution alone does not grant permission to republish copyrighted reporting or photographs, and a prototype must not imply that ingestion, verification, translation, or rights clearance is live.

**How to apply:** Keep local ranking and interest controls functional. When adding provider-backed stories, require commercial-use-compatible source agreements, original-reporting links, duplicate clustering, verification metadata, moderation, and an image-rights layer before presenting the pipeline as production-ready.
