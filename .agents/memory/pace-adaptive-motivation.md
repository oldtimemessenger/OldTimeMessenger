---
name: Pace adaptive motivation
description: Product boundaries for Pace's silent game-like motivation engine.
---

Start Pace motivation with a deterministic, private on-device rival that adapts its win, chase, recovery, and finish states from the current activity's real metrics. Keep every virtual competitor explicitly synthetic.

**Why:** Pace currently has elapsed time, distance, route data, and Strength exercise logs, but no reliable heart-rate or wearable stream. A local engine responds immediately, works offline, and avoids inventing social proof or sending private workout telemetry to a model.

**How to apply:** Use route pace for distance, ride, skate, and swim activities; use exercise volume for Strength; use elapsed-time consistency for Yoga and Dance. Personal learning may be added later from completed sessions, but virtual rivals must never appear as real users, activity, or leaderboard results.
